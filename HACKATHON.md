# 48-hour delivery brief

## Demo story

“Find chicken biryani for three people under ₹800, arriving before 8 PM, and remind Rahul.”

NILA should understand the request, compare mock providers, generate decision cards, prepare the next action and stop for confirmation. The presenter must state that provider data and ordering are simulated.

## Supporting commands

- “What is 18% of 4250?”
- “Set an alarm for 6:30 AM.”
- “Remember that I prefer vegetarian food on Fridays.”
- “Open YouTube.”

## Done means

- The APK builds from a clean clone.
- It installs and launches through the PowerShell script.
- The four supporting commands work without network access.
- The food flow is deterministic and never accidentally performs a real transaction.
- A backup demo video exists outside the repository.

## Claims

Say “launcher prototype,” “mock providers,” and “MCP-ready capability model.” Do not say “complete OS,” “live ONDC ordering,” “fully on-device model,” or “all apps supported” unless those facts become demonstrably true.
