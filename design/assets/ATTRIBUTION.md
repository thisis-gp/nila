# Prototype asset attribution

[Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), distributed under
CC0 1.0. Product names and trademarks remain the property of their respective
owners. They appear only to demonstrate NILA's app-redirect interface.

for NILA and are embedded as editable SVG markup in the prototype.
